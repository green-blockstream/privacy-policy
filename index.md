Privacy Policy – Green Blockstream Wallet

1. Overview
Green Blockstream Wallet is designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, store, or transmit your private keys, seed phrases, or personal financial data to our servers.

2. Local Storage Only
All wallet data, encrypted keys, and transaction history are stored exclusively on your device. We have no access to this information. Your private keys never leave your browser.

3. What We Don't Collect
- Private keys or seed phrases
- Personal identification information
- Email addresses or contact information
- IP addresses or browsing history
- Financial transaction details (beyond what you see locally)

4. What We May Track (Optional)
- Airdrop eligibility events (anonymized, encoded data only)
- Extension version and crash reports for improvement purposes
- Feature usage analytics via opt-in telemetry

5. Blockchain Interactions
When you interact with blockchain networks (Bitcoin, Liquid, Polygon), you connect directly to public blockchain APIs. These connections may log your IP address as per those services' policies. We recommend reviewing Blockstream and public RPC provider privacy policies.

6. Third-Party Services
- Hardware wallet integration (Blockstream Jade) operates independently
- Web3 providers on visited websites handle their own data policies
- Blockchain explorers and RPC endpoints have separate privacy terms

7. Data Security
Wallet data is encrypted locally. We recommend:
- Using strong passwords
- Backing up seed phrases securely offline
- Keeping your browser and extension updated

8. Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy. Continued use constitutes acceptance of changes.

9. Contact
For privacy concerns, contact us through official Blockstream channels.
